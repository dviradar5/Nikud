# Offline vocalizer and word forms

- **Nakdimon** by Elazar Gershuni and Yuval Pinter. [Code and model](https://github.com/elazarg/nakdimon), distributed by the authors under the [MIT license](https://github.com/elazarg/nakdimon/blob/master/LICENSE). The model is embedded in `nakdimon==0.2.1`. [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2022.findings-naacl.75/).
- **UniMorph Hebrew** by Omer Goldman, sourced from Wiktionary. `heb_voc.tsv` is the unmodified [heb_voc file](https://github.com/unimorph/heb/blob/master/heb_voc), SHA-256 `063e0e3624812d815bacc7b29f0fa6d299c3ee4dd509001c9702b345e813d1bd`, licensed [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The app indexes the pointed forms at runtime.
- **Popup glosses** for common words are written for Nikud. Other labels use UniMorph lemmas and grammatical tags, when available.
- **Reviewed names and grammatical labels.** Nikud adds its own concise definitions and meaning-specific grammatical labels. Biblical name spellings are checked against the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible. Selected common and technical spellings are checked against the [Academy terminology database](https://terms.hebrew-academy.org.il/), including [עִפָּרוֹן](https://terms.hebrew-academy.org.il/munnah/7397_1/), [טֵלֵפוֹן](https://terms.hebrew-academy.org.il/munnah/13606_1/), and [אַסְטֵלָה](https://terms.hebrew-academy.org.il/munnah/50869_1/). Definitions are written for Nikud.
- **Biblical fallback forms.** Original work of the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible available at https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb, licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). `biblical_forms.py` was generated from [revision db6c5c9](https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb/tree/db6c5c9b46b8f39346f10915ddb1a70dcd429901/wlc). The generator removes cantillation and meteg and selects recurring forms. Labels identify the register and source, and the Biblical list provides forms when the main lexicon has no entry for a spelling.
- **Shift Happens** by Elaad Adar inspired the Windows tray and shortcut workflow. [Project](https://github.com/Elaad-Adar/shift-happens).
